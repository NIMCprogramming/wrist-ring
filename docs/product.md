# Product rules

## Version 1

1. Keep the phone's normal call screen.
2. Do not change watch sound or vibration settings.
3. When the paired watch is on wrist, silence only the phone ringtone for the
   incoming call.
4. When the watch is off wrist, use the phone's existing ringtone mode and
   volume. Do not force the phone to loud mode.
5. When watch state is unknown or the watch is disconnected, let the phone
   ring normally.
6. Keep all data on the phone and watch. Do not use internet access, accounts,
   analytics, or a server.
7. Use Android's call-screening role. Do not replace the default call screen.
8. Ask for Contacts permission only if a device test proves it is required for
   calls from saved contacts.

## Not in version 1

- Other watch brands or phone models.
- Caller exceptions.
- Cloud services.
- Changing watch alert settings.
- A manual switch.
